Bring a specific planning question and available GIS data. We agree the assessment scope, review criteria and price before work begins.

One landscape. Three comparable options.

  1. Establish the inputs. Review the boundary, road and waterway networks, forest coverage, source dates and data gaps.
  2. Compare agreed scenarios. Map the proposed retention zones and forest within them. Keep road surfaces and open water distinct from retained forest.
  3. Review the result together. Receive maps, a comparison spreadsheet, a printable report and the inputs needed to reproduce the assessment.

Open methods. Portable work.

The application is MIT licensed and can run independently. Viridis's proposed paid service adds data preparation, managed operation, assessment delivery and support. Your maps and assessment records can be exported.

What this assessment establishes

This pilot provides geometric screening. It does not certify ecological viability, regulatory compliance, carbon credits or an optimal forest plan. Source completeness checks do not independently verify a dataset. Appropriate field review remains part of making an operational decision.
