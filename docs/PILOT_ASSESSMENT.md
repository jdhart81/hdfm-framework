# Viridis landscape assessment pilot

## Proposed customer

A forestry or conservation practitioner with an existing planning question and GIS inputs. This is an initial customer hypothesis, not evidence of paid demand.

## Agreed scope before work begins

Record the decision to support, planning boundary, source availability and reuse permissions, responsible reviewer, three proposed parameter scenarios, delivery format, acceptance criteria, price and delivery date. No payment or service commitment is implied by this document.

## Deliverable

A source inventory, mapped roads and waterways, three comparable screening scenarios, maps and area summaries, a reproducible input/results record, and a review of assumptions and data gaps. The reviewer should be able to reproduce the result and understand why scenarios differ.

## Exclusions

No certification of legal compliance, forest yield, carbon credits, population viability, species movement or ecological optimality. Field review and appropriate professional judgment remain necessary. Advanced research algorithms are not part of the pilot service.

## Success evidence

Did the intended user find the assessment useful for a stated decision? What preparation time did it save, and against what baseline? Was the agreed deliverable accepted and paid for? Did the user independently request another assessment? Record these separately; traffic, demonstrations and software tests are not revenue or adoption.
