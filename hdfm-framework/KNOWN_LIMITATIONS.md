# Known limitations

Updated 2026-09-13. Earlier completion percentages and global-optimality claims have been withdrawn from this status page. Implemented interfaces are not evidence of scientific validity.

The authoritative current assessment is [Research status and claim boundaries](../docs/RESEARCH_STATUS.md).

High-priority unresolved issues include movement normalization and population-size interpretation. Hectare/square-meter budgets, solver failure handling and loss of optimized widths were fixed on 2026-10-03; results now carry `success` and `message`. The minimum spanning tree minimizes summed edge length; it does not necessarily minimize the composite entropy score.

The separate [web application](../web/README.md) provides bounded geometric screening with explicit roads, waterways, boundary and forest inputs. It does not use these experimental optimizers or claim genetic, climate or ecological outcomes.
